# Mahjong Club @ UVA 🀄

**Your seat at the table is waiting.**

We're a student-run club at the University of Virginia, bringing people together through mahjong.
Whether you grew up around the game or have never played a hand, you're welcome at our table.
We mainly play Fuzhou-style mahjong, and we'll teach you as we play.
